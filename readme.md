AUTON NAMING CONVENTIONS:
[START SIDE]-[COLLECTION TYPE (HALF/FULL/ALL)]-[ACTION (SHOOT/FEED/COMBO)]-[CLIMB?]

Examples:

Start left trench, collect half of our side of the middle, shoot what we collected, climb
Left-Half-Shoot-Climb

Start left trench, feed half of our side of the middle, climb
Left-Half-Feed-Climb

Start right trench, feed what we can't hold in the middle and collect the rest, shoot, climb 
Right-Full-Combo-Climb

Start left trench, feed all of the middle (both sides), climb
Left-All-Feed-Climb

Start left trench, feed both sides that we can't hold and collect the rest, shoot, climb
Right-All-Combo-Climb
